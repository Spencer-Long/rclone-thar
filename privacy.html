Privacy policy for rclone thar

Last updated November 24, 2026.

rclone thar is used only by its owner. It accesses Google Drive only to list and copy files the owner already has access to, onto a computer the owner controls.

The app collects no personal data. It keeps no Google user data anywhere except on the owner's own computer, and it does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone.

Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions: contact the owner through the GitHub account that publishes this page.
